# LoopLocal feature evidence matrix — 2026-09-26

This is an inventory of **our implementation**, not a Fidelix parity score. [Fidelix's public site](https://fidelix.ma/) advertises the feature categories below; [its demo join page](https://fidelix.ma/join/demo) visibly presents a customer form and reward rule. Authenticated Fidelix behavior is unknown. Its [privacy](https://fidelix.ma/confidentialite) and [terms](https://fidelix.ma/conditions) pages are public descriptions of its service, not instructions or compliance approval for LoopLocal. LoopLocal uses original code, copy, components and branding. Its own requirements prohibit rewarded reviews and automatic invitations from an import.

`LOCAL` means our synthetic PostgreSQL/Chrome/Mailpit exercise. `FIXTURE` means a fake provider or signing fixture. `REAL_PROVIDER` and `PHYSICAL_DEVICE` are **NOT RUN**. Mailpit acceptance does not establish external delivery, opens, sales uplift or legal permission. A recorded checkout is one distinct unreversed staff-award request; it does not prove a physical visit. The campaign outbox is at least once if Mailpit captures a message and the database transaction fails before commit.
