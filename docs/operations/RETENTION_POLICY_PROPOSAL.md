## Approved decisions

The owner approved all five choices below with “approve all five” on 15 October 2026:

1. **Delete closed-card history instead of retaining an allegedly anonymous ledger.** Delete the complete card record set within 24 hours when no Wallet work remains; allow at most seven days from closure for Wallet revocation. Do not retain individual loyalty transactions for general analytics or an assumed tax obligation. A documented legal hold is the only exception.
2. **Use the category schedule below.** Keep encrypted recovery backups for 14 days, with a separate 30-day erasure manifest so a restore cannot silently resurrect deleted records. Retain a minimal email suppression hash for three years to honor objections; this is a disclosed personal-data exception, not anonymous data.
3. **Export all personal data attributable to the requested card.** Require fresh control of a stored email address before disclosing contact/recovery details. A contactless card uses its private card credential. Provide an operator-assisted route where a person cannot use those credentials. Exporting one card does not disclose other cards sharing an email or the other party to a referral.
4. **Merchant deletion ends the whole tenant's service.** Require a recently authenticated owner, an explicit tenant confirmation, and an opportunity to download an export first. Revoke access immediately and complete local tenant deletion within seven days. Keep only separately classified statutory business records and any specific legal hold.
5. **Do not invent loyalty-balance expiry terms.** Automatically close a zero-balance card after 24 months without a confirmed loyalty transaction or explicit customer card use. Flag nonzero-balance cards at 24 months for monthly merchant review; their termination/expiry terms require a separate contractual decision. Reading a pass image, provider refresh, or staff report does not renew activity.

Approving these choices approves implementation and synthetic tests only. Production activation, migrations, schedules, provider requests, and spending remain separate operator steps.

## Scope and legal interpretation to confirm

The initial scope assumes a German merchant and EU processing. Confirm the actual controller/processor roles and merchant agreement before launch. Proposed operational periods below are product choices, not periods prescribed by GDPR. Pseudonymous membership IDs, transaction times, email hashes, pass IDs, and linked ledger rows remain potentially personal data. Removing an email alone is not full erasure. GDPR requires purpose-based storage limitation, access/erasure processes, appropriate security, and breach handling; exceptions to erasure must be justified for the specific records. See [GDPR Articles 5, 12, 15, 17, 20, 28, 32–34](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng).

Do not classify all loyalty events as accounting records by default. A German accountant must determine whether particular purchase/reward records are required tax evidence. Actual accounting books generally have a ten-year period, accounting vouchers eight years, and qualifying business correspondence six years, normally counted from year-end; litigation/audit exceptions can extend the applicable period. Only the applicable categories belong in the restricted statutory archive. See [HGB §257](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/hgb/__257.html) and [AO §147](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ao_1977/__147.html). Current `billing_invoices` rows are expressly synthetic fixtures, not valid tax invoices.

## Approved retention schedule

All durations use UTC elapsed time unless a statutory calendar-year rule applies. The pruning job, scheduled every five minutes, acts on rows whose deadline is reached, using bounded batches and an exclusive job lock. An overdue purge is a monitored failure, not an automatic extension of the stated policy. Closure is distinct from completed erasure.

## Export contents and authorization

The full export is versioned JSON from one read-only repeatable-read snapshot: membership identifiers that are not credentials, balance and dates; merchant/program/rule context; every own ledger entry and reversal; redemption and purchase snapshots; stored contact and verification status; consent decisions; security/reissue events; own NFC/offline outcomes; own referral role/bonus/outcome without the counterparty identity; campaign/automation participation and delivery status; Wallet provider/status/dates; and relevant retention/erasure exceptions. Include purposes, recipients/processor categories, retention periods and the request route as accompanying metadata.

Never include bearer or scan credentials, session/reset tokens, password hashes, challenge hashes, push endpoint secrets, private keys, other customers' records, or unnecessary staff identifiers. Describe withheld credentials by category; withholding secrets is not a reason to omit the underlying event. Avoid logging export content. Generate on demand without storing a server-side downloadable copy. Return no partial attachment if any category fails. Large exports must have a complete bounded generation path, not silent truncation.

The current activity download remains clearly labelled as such until the complete flow exists. A fresh one-time challenge, expiring after ten minutes and usable once, verifies stored-email control before contact-rich export. For an unverified stored address or lost credentials, use an operator-assisted identity assessment proportionate to the request; do not expose an address merely because it was typed onto a card. The operator must also handle access requests after closure and explain any residual data/hold. Target response within seven days; the applicable statutory response clock is monitored separately.

## Ledger and concurrency rules

Closure takes the same membership lock used by award/redeem, suppresses pending effects and retires authorization atomically. A purge locks the closed membership, checks the approved deadline and hold state, and deletes its dependent graph in one transaction. It removes all of that card's ledger entries, redemptions and reversal links together; it does not reset a balance while leaving contradictory entries or remove a foreign customer's bonus. Every surviving membership must still equal the sum of its own ledger deltas. Cross-tenant references must remain valid.

Keep ordinary ledger UPDATE/DELETE forbidden. Any maintenance exception must be restricted to a dedicated purge role or narrowly scoped database procedure, with fixed search path, no PUBLIC execution, explicit closed-subject predicates, bounded work and a sanitized audit receipt. A caller-set session flag alone is not authorization. Do not disable triggers globally. Audit retention requires the same narrow maintenance boundary. Migration rollback may remove unused structures; it cannot recover erased data. Restoring deleted data to public service is not a valid rollback.

A failure rolls back the entire purge. Concurrent award, redemption, reissue, contact change, email recovery, campaign delivery and Wallet issuance/revocation must not resurrect the subject. Export snapshots already authorized before closure may finish; after closure, old bearer requests fail. Avoid unbounded transactions that delay other merchants.

## Merchant deletion and legal holds

Owner authentication must be no older than ten minutes, with reauthentication where needed; managers and cashiers cannot initiate tenant deletion. Show the tenant identity, export option, service shutdown, balance consequences and retention exceptions before confirmation. Immediately stop enrollment, earning/redemption, automation/campaigns and provider issuance for that tenant, and revoke all staff sessions/access. This does not authorize a real payment-provider cancellation from a local fixture; the operator must reconcile any actual contract separately.

Every hold names the legal basis, exact categories/subjects, authorized custodian, reason, start, expiry/review date and release condition. Review every 30 days. Restrict held records from ordinary operations and marketing. Do not use a blanket 'possible dispute' hold for all merchants. Tell the requester what remains and why, subject to lawful restrictions. The owner/accountant must classify any genuine statutory invoices before tenant purge is enabled; otherwise affected tenant deletion remains pending with an explicit reason.

## Wallet and backup limitations

Remote revocation means issuing a voided Apple pass/update or deactivating a Google object, then tracking the actual provider result. A provider acknowledgment does not prove an offline device fetched the update. The server must reject every old credential immediately regardless. An app cannot guarantee removal of a saved pass from a customer's device. After the seven-day local retention deadline, unresolved remote cleanup is an operator case and must not be reported as successful erasure. See [Apple pass update protocol](https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/documentation/UserExperience/Conceptual/PassKit_PG/Updating.html) and [Google loyalty object state](https://developers.google.com/wallet/reference/rest/v1/loyaltyobject).

Keep the private backup decryption key outside the production runtime, with named primary and alternate custodians. The restore drill for this task uses only newly generated synthetic data and a temporary key, never a real backup. A production restore requires its own authorization and an empty isolated target; all workers, email, push and Wallet integrations stay off until invariant checks and the independently stored erasure manifest have been applied.
