## A. Critical path — shortest credible order

1. **Start external lead times now:** owner/legal supplies the consolidated inputs below; reserve provider accounts, test devices, secure tag/provisioner and production destination without purchases until costs are approved. Choose the manual invoice route if operator/legal review accepts it; this avoids a hosted billing integration for v1.
2. **Implement the missing independent cores concurrently:** secure NFC verifier/atomic award, secure recovery, production campaign adapter/events and real manual billing activation. Preserve the existing ledger, outbox, QR and merchant UI. Complete local security and regression tests for each.
3. **Establish always-on production-equivalent infrastructure:** private DB/web/worker/secrets, monitoring, redacted proxy logs, migrations and encrypted restore drill. Provide reviewed narrow Apple callback and Google asset endpoints; retain separate protected test access.
4. **Run real integration evidence:** approved NFC provisioning on iPhone/Android; Apple and Google install/scan/update; verified external promotional test; real recovery and invoice/entitlement. Fix only observed release defects.
5. **Approve and publish root website, then exercise the full golden path** with designated non-production identities. Run release regression, operational drill and owner/legal signoff. Mark each gate `PASS` only with recorded evidence for this revision.

## B. Parallel work

NFC server work, recovery, real delivery adapter, manual billing process and production hosting can proceed together. Apple and Google setup/tests are separate tracks. Operator/legal texts, approved pricing/contact and provider account lead times can progress throughout. The final golden path waits for all eight gate dependencies; marketing publication waits for its own legal/contact approval.

## C. Consolidated owner inputs

1. **Production destination and spending:** approved app hostname, hosting/DB/backup/monitoring operator, access owners and cost ceiling; domain/DNS approval for `getloynara.com` and the app origin.
2. **Devices and NFC:** designated iPhone/Android models and testers; approved fixed-stamp rule/cooldown; genuine NTAG 424 DNA tag and controlled provisioning/key custodian.
3. **Wallet:** Apple Developer team, Pass Type ID/certificate provisioned securely; Google issuer, authorized service account and Demo Mode tester; exact provider-facing callback/asset URLs and route approval. Keys never go in chat or Git.
4. **Campaigns and recovery:** approved external mail provider/sender/domain, controlled real recipient, channel/purpose consent and recovery wording, unsubscribe destination, one specific promotional test/send approval.
5. **Operator and payment:** legal identity, operating permission, postal/support contact, approved offer and cancellation terms, VAT/tax/invoice treatment, explicit choice of manual invoice or hosted billing, processor/subprocessor list, merchant terms/DPA where applicable, privacy/consent/retention/deletion policy and Impressum, with legal-adviser review where needed.

## E. Release stop rule

When **all eight gates are `PASS` for the same release candidate**, v1 scope closes. New ideas go to [POST_V1.md](POST_V1.md) and cannot delay launch unless they uncover a security, legal, transaction-integrity or severe usability defect. A local fixture, browser emulation, owner-reported QR scan or public audit site never upgrades an unrelated gate to `PASS`.
