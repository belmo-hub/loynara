The recording.
Step by step.

Local application, synthetic data, simulated camera, no audio.

The original recording shows the application in German. This is its English transcript.

  1. Owner dashboard: two synthetic cards and confirmed activity.
  2. Programme: five stamps for a coffee, plus another reward with a separate redemption cost.
  3. Join: review the rules and open a new browser card.
  4. Reopen the card: use its private link; the initial balance is zero stamps.
  5. Checkout: QR recognition with a simulated camera. Confirm one stamp first, then explicitly confirm four more.
  6. Choose a reward and confirm redemption. The balance goes from five to zero.
  7. Customer card: updated balance and activity.
  8. Owner dashboard: confirmed transactions are visible.
  9. Activity and audience: the number of active cards rises from one to two.
  10. Campaign: audience, preview and a scheduled message.
  11. Mailpit: one locally captured message to a synthetic, verified contact with recorded consent. No external delivery.
  12. Referral: a confirmed qualifying event appears in the report.
  13. Team and locations: assigned roles and separate programmes.
  14. Offline earning: pending; a stamp counts only after server acceptance.
  15. Plan status: a saved draft, with no payment.

The interactive demo deliberately uses its own eight-stamp rule. Both demonstrations are examples, not evidence of revenue results.
