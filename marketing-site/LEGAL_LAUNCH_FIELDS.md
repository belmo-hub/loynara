# Fields needed before public commercial activation

- Operator's full legal name and business form.
- Serviceable postal address and contact email.
- Register name/number and VAT ID, if applicable to this operator.
- Responsible person and any profession-specific details, if legally applicable.
- Approved Impressum and privacy text for the actual public static site, including the Cloudflare hosting arrangement and contact channel.
- Approved contact destination/storage and data handling before enabling the form; no working inbox is assumed.
- Final commercial offer, setup scope, contract duration and cancellation terms. The €99 including month one package remains a private draft, distinct from the existing setup-plus-monthly proposals.
- Approval for the exact Pages project, apex DNS assignment and optional www redirect after reviewing the completed site.

The current private static site transmits no form input and uses no trackers. The optional conversation note is a local download; submission stays disabled. These fields require the owner's authoritative information and legal review; they are not inferred from the domain account.
