# 09 · 30-day content calendar

**Day 1 is the first day the Loynara Instagram/TikTok account shows a valid Impressum (O-001).** Before that, nothing is published. The founder can pre-produce the first 10 pieces during field weeks 1–2 (most need only the demo merchant from D-3, plus a phone).

**Rhythm:**
- 4 feed posts per week: Reel/TikTok cross-posted, plus 1 static or carousel.
- Stories daily (5 minutes), real numbers only.
- Batch-produce on day 5 of each field week (see [README](README.md) week plan).

**Rules:** every piece passes the claim check in [02-positioning-angles.md](02-positioning-angles.md). Stories with field numbers must be true and consistent with `pipeline.csv`.

**Production checklist per video:**
1. „Beispiel“ label is visible on every card and owner screen.
2. „Gestellte Szene“ is visible on staged moments.
3. No NFC, Wallet, logo or messaging is shown as available.
4. No shop is identifiable without written permission.
5. Captions are burned in, since most viewers watch muted.
6. The CTA keyword is in both the caption and the video.
