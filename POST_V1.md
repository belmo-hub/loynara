# Post-v1 backlog boundary

These do not block the eight gates in [V1_RELEASE_GATES.md](V1_RELEASE_GATES.md): native customer or merchant apps; Apple VAS; Google Smart Tap terminals; Tap to Pay loyalty integration; POS integrations; SMS; WhatsApp Business automation; advanced generative AI adviser; white-label/reseller portal; Morocco launch; full Arabic; France-specific production launch; predictive ML; advanced gamification; enterprise API expansion.

Existing local work may remain in the repository, but no new work on these items enters v1 unless it fixes a security, legal, transaction-integrity or severe usability defect in the release gates.
